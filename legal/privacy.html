Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

At TrendingDays Tools Hub (accessible via trendingdays.com), we hold your digital privacy in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we ensure your data remains protected while using our suite of digital utilities.

1. The "Zero-Data" Processing Commitment

The core philosophy of TrendingDays is built on local execution. Unlike traditional web applications that process user data on a remote server, our tools—including the Loan Calculator, Password Generator, and BMI Tracker—utilize Client-Side JavaScript logic. This means all mathematical calculations and data inputs happen exclusively within your browser's memory. We do not transmit, store, or see your private inputs. Once you close your browser tab, the data is wiped from your local session.

2. Standard Web Server Log Files

Like all professional websites, TrendingDays utilizes standard log files provided by our secure hosting infrastructure. These logs capture non-identifying technical data, including Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser versions, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring/exit pages. This information is used strictly for technical maintenance, security monitoring, and analyzing broad traffic trends to improve site performance. This data is never linked to personally identifiable information.

3. Cookies and Advertising Standards

TrendingDays uses 'cookies' to store visitor preferences and optimize your experience. Furthermore, we may partner with third-party vendors, such as Google AdSense, to serve advertisements. Google utilizes the DART cookie to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other websites on the internet. You may choose to decline the use of personalized advertising cookies by visiting the Google Ad Settings or by utilizing an "Incognito" browser mode.

4. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We are committed to full compliance with global data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Users have the right to request information about data collection. However, because our architecture is designed to not collect personal inputs from our tools, we do not maintain a database of your calculations, health scores, or financial records to disclose or sell.

5. Third-Party Links and Security

Our website may contain links to external sites. We advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit. While we provide a secure environment at trendingdays.com, we have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or privacy practices of any third-party services or websites linked in our footer or tools.

6. Children's Privacy

TrendingDays does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately so we can perform the necessary steps to remove such information from our technical logs.
